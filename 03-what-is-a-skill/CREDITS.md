# Credits

## Voice, music and sound effects
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs premade voice "Chris" (`eleven_multilingual_v2`), one line per scene; the scenes are timed to the lines.
- **Music:** the same ElevenLabs-generated track as post 02 (125 BPM), cut at bar lines and looped once under the end card.
- **Sound effects:** ElevenLabs-generated card flip, file whoosh, match ding and tick, plus the post 02 pops, whooshes and chime.
- **Mix:** music sidechained about -20 dB under the voice, effects about -6 dB under it, mastered to -14 LUFS.
