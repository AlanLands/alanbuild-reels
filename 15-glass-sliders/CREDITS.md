# Credits

## Music and sound effects
All audio in this reel was generated with ElevenLabs: the music bed ("dark teal ambient electronic", 86 BPM, A minor,
2026-11-13) and the sound effects (taps, pops, whooshes, chime, 2026-10-30), then edited to the picture with the real-sound
method: effects placed on their cue frames (a rising pop on every slider name), mixed to -14 LUFS. Voiceover: ElevenLabs stock
voice "Chris".

## Fonts
The reel uses Apple's SF and SF Mono, which are not redistributable, so `assets/fonts/` is not in this repo; `sliders.html` uses
the system font.
